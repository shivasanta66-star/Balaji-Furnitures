Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

1. Ready-stock furniture

You can cancel within 48 hours of payment, as long as the item has not been dispatched, and get a full refund. Orders cannot be cancelled after dispatch or delivery.

2. Custom / made-to-measure furniture

If you cancel before we start making your piece, we refund the full advance. Once work has started, the advance is non-refundable, because the piece is made to your size and choice.

3. Damaged or wrong item

Please check your furniture when it is delivered. If it is damaged or not what you ordered, tell us within 24 hours of delivery with photos on WhatsApp. We will repair, replace or refund it.

4. If we cannot supply

If we cannot supply the item you paid for, we refund the full amount.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the same payment method you used (UPI, card or bank account) within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take a few extra days to show it.

6. How to ask for a refund

Call or WhatsApp us with your name, phone number, payment date and amount (or the Razorpay payment ID from your receipt).
