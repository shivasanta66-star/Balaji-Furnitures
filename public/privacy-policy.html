Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Balaji Furnitures respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. What we collect

When you send an enquiry, message us or pay online, we may collect your name, phone number, email address and delivery address.

2. Payment details

Online payments are handled by Razorpay. Your card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay's secure page and are not seen or stored by us. We only receive the payment status, amount and your contact details.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiries
  • To confirm your order and payment
  • To arrange delivery and after-sale service
  • To tell you about offers, only if you agree (you can ask us to stop at any time)

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with the payment gateway, our delivery staff, or when the law requires it.

5. Security

This website uses a secure (HTTPS) connection and we take reasonable care to keep your information safe.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information by contacting us using the details below.
