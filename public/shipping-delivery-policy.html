Delivery Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

1. Where we deliver

We deliver to Jharigam, Umerkote, Raighar, Chandahandi, Papadahandi, Nabarangpur, Kosagumuda, Tentulikhunti and nearby villages. If your village is not listed, ask us on WhatsApp.

2. Delivery time

  • Ready-stock items: usually within 2–5 days of payment.
  • Custom pieces: usually ready in 2–3 weeks, as agreed when you order.

We call you before delivery to fix a convenient time.

3. Delivery charges

Local delivery is free. For far-off locations, any charge is told to you before you pay.

4. Assembly

Delivery and assembly are free. Our team sets up your furniture at your home when it is delivered.

5. Store pickup

You can also collect your furniture from our showroom on Main Road, Jharigam.

6. Delays

Delivery may sometimes be delayed by weather, festivals or other reasons outside our control. We will keep you informed.
